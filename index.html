A tool is an invitation, not a treatment on its own. Qualified therapists adapt materials and interactions to each child’s interests, communication and sensory preferences.

Child-led, thoughtfully adapted

Start with the child.

Offer choices, welcome different forms of communication, and make “stop” or “take a break” easy to express. Adjust sound, texture, movement and complexity to the individual.

These are illustrative uses, not session prescriptions or claims of proven benefit for each tool. Art classes, music lessons and recreational dance can be valuable; professional therapy also involves a therapeutic relationship and individual goals.

Digital systems and robots described here include research prototypes. They are not standard equipment or replacements for a therapist.
